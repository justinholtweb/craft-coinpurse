# Plugin Store promo images

Marketing images for the Coin Purse listing on the Craft Plugin Store, rendered in the same theme as
the plugin's marketing page at
[justinholt.com/plugins/craft-coinpurse](https://justinholt.com/plugins/craft-coinpurse).

## Building

```bash
./build.sh          # all slides
./build.sh "2 5"    # just slides 2 and 5
```

Output lands in `out/` as `coinpurse-promo-N.jpg`, 1920×1080 (rendered at 2× in headless Chrome, then
downsampled so the type stays crisp). **Promos are JPEG, never PNG.** Chrome can only write PNG, so
`build.sh` converts with `sips` at quality 90 and deletes the intermediate.

## Slides

| # | Slide | Shows |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | Cover: name, tagline, app icon, price | — |
| 2 | One tap, from the first page they see | the Payment Element's five steps at checkout against tap, sheet, order placed |
| 3 | Every number is the server's | the sheet's round trips to `coinpurse/express/*`, the pending rate, and a moved total refused |
| 4 | The button is signed | the Twig tag, what is and isn't signed, and a tampered item ID answered with a 400 |
| 5 | The cart isn't collateral damage | `items` mode's detached order, `cart` mode's snapshot and restore, `sessions/reap` |
| 6 | Why is there no button? | **real** screenshot of the Diagnostics screen |
| 7 | Stripe, or any other gateway | the two drivers, and the `craft.webPayments.button()` alias |

Slides 3 and 4 quote the code, so they have to agree with it:

- The endpoints are `coinpurse/express/start`, `address`, `shipping` and `pay`
  (`services\Buttons::endpoints()`).
- The refusal on slide 4 is the literal message `ExpressController::requireSignedOptions()` throws.
- The signed list on slide 4 is `ButtonOptions::signablePayload()`: mode, items (id, qty, options,
  note), requestShipping, requestDetails, siteId, gatewayId, expires. Style and `onComplete` are not
  signed.
- The amounts on slide 3 are illustrative. `expected 5600` is minor units because that is what
  the browser posts as `expectedTotal`.

Change any of those and change the slide.

## Palette

Accent `#BA7B4E`, the midpoint of the icon tile's gradient (`#E19F6D` → `#92562E`). `#F0B88A` is the
light accent for anything small on the dark background: eyebrows, check marks, code keys. The tile's
own light end, `#E19F6D`, is a shade too dark for 20px type there. The family's `#ffd166` gold stays;
it sits naturally next to copper and ends the headline gradient. Jersey 20 for display, Inter for
body.

## The watermark is the purse without its clasp

`assets/watermark.svg` is the path from `../src/icon-mask.svg` in white, with the tile stripped as
everywhere in the family. It is also clipped at `y = 27.5`, which removes the two clasp balls: the
watermark is anchored bottom-right and runs off the edge, and at that position and size the balls
landed at the slide's right edge as a pair of dots that read like UI. The coins stay as holes
because the mark is a single traced path. It is switched off on the cover, where it sat behind the
icon like a shadow.

`assets/icon.svg` is a straight copy of `../src/icon.svg`. Re-copy it whenever the icon changes.

## Screenshots

`shots/` holds the two captures, also used on the marketing page
(`~/Sites/justinholt/web/images/plugins/screenshots/craft-coinpurse/`). Both were captured from the
plugin-testing harness (`~/Sites/plugin-shots/specs/coinpurse.json`, 1500 CSS px wide at 2×):

- `coinpurse-diagnostics.png`: Coin Purse → Diagnostics, on the harness's Dummy gateway with the
  direct driver and no Apple or Google merchant details, which is why most of it is red. That is
  the screen doing its job.
- `coinpurse-settings.png`: the top of Coin Purse → Settings (gateway, driver, wallets)

Cropped with `sips --cropToHeightWidth H W --cropOffset 1 1`. An offset of `0 0` is ignored and
gives a *centred* crop; `1 1` crops from the top.

`fonts.css` is generated by `build.sh` and gitignored; don't edit it.
