Customers close sheets

The cart isn't
collateral
damage

A sheet has to write an address onto an order to price it. Coin Purse makes sure nothing sticks.

items: [ … ]Buy now

Builds a detached order with its own number, never put in the session. The cart they've been filling for twenty minutes isn't touched.

Uses the real cart, so coupons, gift notes and custom fields come along. Before the sheet opens it snapshots

addressshipping methodemail

and puts them back on cancel, failure or expiry. A scheduled coinpurse/sessions/reap catches the tabs that just closed.

Two drivers, picked for you

Stripe, or any other gateway

Payments stay your gateway's. Transactions, status emails, captures and refunds are Commerce's own code either way.

Stripe Express Checkout Elementcraftcms/commerce-stripeCommerce creates the PaymentIntent, the browser confirms it with the wallet, Commerce finishes it. Stripe holds the Apple Pay certificate and the domain verification.
Apple Pay JS and Google Pay APIEvery other Commerce gatewaySpoken directly. Both wallets encrypt the token to your gateway, so it passes through the site opaque and lands on the gateway's own payment form.

Coming from Web Payments? Same option names, and craft.webPayments.button() keeps working.
